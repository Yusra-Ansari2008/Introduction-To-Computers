## Introduction 

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data as input, processes it according to instructions, stores information, and produces useful output.

Computers are used in many areas such as education, business, communication, science, engineering, healthcare, and entertainment.

## The basic working process of a computer can be represented as:

**Input → Processing → Storage → Output**
## Types of Computers

Computers can be classified into different types according to their size, processing capability, and intended use.

### 1. Supercomputers

Supercomputers are extremely powerful computers designed to perform complex calculations at very high speeds. They are used in scientific research, simulations, weather modelling, and other computationally demanding tasks.

### 2. Mainframe Computers

Mainframe computers are designed to handle large amounts of data and support many users or transactions at the same time. They are commonly used by large organizations.

### 3. Minicomputers

Minicomputers were smaller than traditional mainframe computers and were used for organizational and departmental computing.

### 4. Personal Computers

Personal computers are designed mainly for individual users. Desktop computers and laptops are common examples. These makes the every day life so easy for an individual. 

### 5. Mobile Computers

Mobile computers are portable devices that allow users to perform computing tasks while moving from one place to another. They are easy to carry and move . Laptops, tablets, and smartphones are examples.
### Types of Computers at a Glance

| Type | Main Purpose | Example Use |
|---|---|---|
| Supercomputer | Complex calculations | Scientific research |
| Mainframe | Large-scale data processing | Banking systems |
| Minicomputer | Department-level computing | Organizational systems |
| Personal Computer | Individual use | Education and office work |
| Mobile Computer | Portable computing | Communication and learning |
